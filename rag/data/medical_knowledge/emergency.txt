Topic: Medical Emergencies and First Aid (الطوارئ الطبية والإسعافات الأولية)
Category: Emergency Medicine

--- Anaphylaxis (صدمة الحساسية) ---
Causes: Food (peanuts, shellfish), insect stings, medications (penicillin), latex
Symptoms: Swelling of face/throat, difficulty breathing, rapid pulse, drop in blood pressure, hives, dizziness
EMERGENCY Treatment:
1. Epinephrine (Adrenaline) IM injection — FIRST and most important step
2. Call emergency services
3. Lay patient flat, elevate legs
4. Oxygen if available
5. IV fluids, antihistamines, steroids (secondary)
EpiPen: 0.3 mg adults, 0.15 mg children. Inject into outer thigh.

--- Hypoglycemia (انخفاض السكر في الدم) ---
Definition: Blood glucose < 70 mg/dL
Causes: Excess insulin, missed meals, excessive exercise, alcohol
Symptoms:
- Mild: Shakiness, sweating, hunger, fast heartbeat, anxiety
- Severe: Confusion, seizures, loss of consciousness
Treatment:
- Conscious: 15-20 g fast-acting sugar (juice, glucose tablets, candy), recheck in 15 min
- Unconscious: Glucagon injection, IV dextrose. DO NOT give food/drink by mouth.
- Rule of 15: 15g sugar → wait 15 min → recheck → repeat if still low

--- Burns (الحروق) ---
Degrees:
- First degree: Red skin, pain, no blisters (sunburn). Treat: Cool water, aloe vera, Paracetamol
- Second degree: Blisters, severe pain, red/mottled. Treat: Cool water 10-20 min, don't pop blisters, dress loosely
- Third degree: White/charred, painless (nerve damage). EMERGENCY: Hospital, may need grafting
General: Cool running water 10-20 min, remove jewelry, cover with clean non-stick dressing
NEVER: Apply ice, butter, toothpaste. Don't remove stuck clothing.

--- Choking (الاختناق) ---
Adults: Abdominal thrusts (Heimlich maneuver) — stand behind, fist above navel, thrust upward
Infants (< 1 year): 5 back blows + 5 chest thrusts, alternate until clear
If unconscious: CPR, check mouth for object before giving breaths

--- CPR (الإنعاش القلبي الرئوي) ---
When: Person unresponsive and not breathing normally
Steps:
1. Call emergency services
2. 30 chest compressions: Push hard and fast, center of chest, 5-6 cm depth, 100-120/min
3. 2 rescue breaths (if trained)
4. Continue 30:2 ratio until help arrives or AED available
5. Use AED as soon as available — follow voice prompts
Hands-only CPR: Acceptable if untrained — continuous chest compressions

--- Fracture First Aid (إسعاف الكسور) ---
Signs: Pain, swelling, deformity, inability to move, crepitus
First Aid:
- Immobilize the injury (splint in position found)
- Apply ice wrapped in cloth
- Elevate if possible
- Don't try to realign the bone
- Seek medical care
Open fracture (bone visible): Cover with clean dressing, call emergency, don't push bone back

--- Wound Care (العناية بالجروح) ---
Minor cuts: Clean with water, apply pressure to stop bleeding, antiseptic, sterile dressing
Deep wounds: Apply firm pressure, don't remove embedded objects, seek medical care
When to get stitches: Deep cut, > 2 cm, won't stop bleeding, on face/joint, edges gaping
Tetanus: Ensure vaccination is up to date for dirty/deep wounds
