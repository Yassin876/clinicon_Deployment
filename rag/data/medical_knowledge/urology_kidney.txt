Topic: Kidney and Urinary Tract Diseases (أمراض الكلى والمسالك البولية)
Category: Urology / Nephrology

--- Urinary Tract Infection / UTI (التهاب المسالك البولية) ---
Types: Lower (cystitis — bladder), Upper (pyelonephritis — kidney)
Risk Factors: Female gender, sexual activity, pregnancy, diabetes, urinary catheter
Symptoms:
- Cystitis: Burning urination, frequency, urgency, lower abdominal pain, cloudy/bloody urine
- Pyelonephritis: Fever, flank pain, nausea, vomiting + cystitis symptoms
Diagnosis: Urinalysis, urine culture and sensitivity
Treatment:
- Uncomplicated cystitis: Nitrofurantoin 5 days, or Trimethoprim-Sulfamethoxazole 3 days
- Pyelonephritis: Ciprofloxacin 7 days or IV antibiotics if severe
- Increase water intake, urinate after intercourse (prevention)

--- Kidney Stones (حصوات الكلى) ---
Types: Calcium oxalate (most common), Uric acid, Struvite, Cystine
Symptoms: Severe flank pain (renal colic), radiating to groin, hematuria, nausea, vomiting
Diagnosis: Non-contrast CT scan (gold standard), Ultrasound, Urinalysis
Treatment:
- < 5 mm: Likely to pass spontaneously. Hydration, NSAIDs, Tamsulosin (Alpha-blocker)
- 5-10 mm: May need intervention
- > 10 mm or obstructing: ESWL (Extracorporeal Shock Wave Lithotripsy), Ureteroscopy, PCNL
Prevention: Drink 2-3 liters water/day, reduce sodium and animal protein, limit oxalate-rich foods

--- Chronic Kidney Disease / CKD (مرض الكلى المزمن) ---
Definition: Progressive loss of kidney function over months/years
Stages (by GFR):
- Stage 1: GFR >= 90 (normal kidney function but other evidence of damage)
- Stage 2: GFR 60-89 (mild decrease)
- Stage 3: GFR 30-59 (moderate decrease)
- Stage 4: GFR 15-29 (severe decrease)
- Stage 5: GFR < 15 (kidney failure — dialysis or transplant needed)
Causes: Diabetes (most common), Hypertension, Glomerulonephritis
Treatment: Control diabetes and BP, ACE inhibitors/ARBs, low-protein diet, avoid nephrotoxic drugs
Dialysis: Hemodialysis or Peritoneal dialysis when GFR < 10-15

--- Benign Prostatic Hyperplasia / BPH (تضخم البروستاتا) ---
Common in men > 50 years
Symptoms: Weak urine stream, frequency, urgency, nocturia, incomplete emptying, hesitancy
Diagnosis: Digital rectal exam, PSA blood test, Ultrasound, Uroflowmetry
Treatment:
- Mild: Watchful waiting, lifestyle changes
- Alpha-blockers: Tamsulosin, Alfuzosin (relax prostate muscles)
- 5-alpha reductase inhibitors: Finasteride, Dutasteride (shrink prostate)
- Surgery: TURP (transurethral resection) for severe cases
