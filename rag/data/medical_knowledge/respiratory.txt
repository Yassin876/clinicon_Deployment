Topic: Common Respiratory Diseases (أمراض الجهاز التنفسي)
Category: Pulmonology

--- Common Cold (نزلة البرد) ---
Cause: Viral infection (rhinovirus most common)
Symptoms: Runny nose, sneezing, sore throat, mild cough, low-grade fever
Treatment: Rest, fluids, Paracetamol for fever, decongestants, usually resolves in 7-10 days
Note: Antibiotics are NOT effective against viral infections

--- Influenza / Flu (الإنفلونزا) ---
Cause: Influenza virus (A, B, C)
Symptoms: High fever, body aches, severe fatigue, headache, dry cough, sore throat
Treatment: Oseltamivir (Tamiflu) within 48 hours of symptoms, rest, fluids, Paracetamol
Prevention: Annual flu vaccine
Complications: Pneumonia, myocarditis, especially dangerous for elderly and immunocompromised

--- Asthma (الربو) ---
Definition: Chronic inflammatory disease of the airways causing reversible airflow obstruction
Triggers: Allergens, cold air, exercise, smoke, stress, respiratory infections
Symptoms: Wheezing, shortness of breath, chest tightness, cough (especially at night)
Treatment:
- Reliever: Salbutamol (Ventolin) inhaler — for acute attacks
- Controller: Inhaled corticosteroids (Fluticasone, Budesonide) — daily prevention
- Severe: Add long-acting beta-agonists (Salmeterol), Montelukast
- Emergency: Nebulizer, systemic steroids, oxygen

--- Pneumonia (الالتهاب الرئوي) ---
Definition: Infection that inflames air sacs in one or both lungs
Causes: Bacteria (Streptococcus pneumoniae most common), viruses, fungi
Symptoms: Productive cough, fever, chills, shortness of breath, chest pain when breathing
Diagnosis: Chest X-ray, CBC, sputum culture
Treatment:
- Bacterial: Amoxicillin, Azithromycin, or Levofloxacin
- Viral: Supportive care, antivirals if influenza-related
- Severe: Hospitalization, IV antibiotics, oxygen therapy

--- Bronchitis (التهاب الشعب الهوائية) ---
Acute: Usually viral, lasts 1-3 weeks. Treatment: rest, fluids, cough suppressants
Chronic: Part of COPD. Productive cough for at least 3 months in 2 consecutive years
Treatment: Bronchodilators, inhaled steroids, smoking cessation

--- COVID-19 ---
Cause: SARS-CoV-2 virus
Symptoms: Fever, dry cough, fatigue, loss of taste/smell, shortness of breath
Severity: Ranges from asymptomatic to severe pneumonia and ARDS
Treatment: Supportive care, Paxlovid for high-risk patients, oxygen for severe cases
Prevention: Vaccination, hand hygiene, masks in high-risk settings
