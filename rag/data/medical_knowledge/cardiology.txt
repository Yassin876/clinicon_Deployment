Topic: Heart Diseases (أمراض القلب)
Category: Cardiology

--- Coronary Artery Disease (مرض الشريان التاجي) ---
Definition: Narrowing or blockage of coronary arteries due to plaque buildup (atherosclerosis)
Risk Factors: Smoking, hypertension, diabetes, high cholesterol, obesity, family history
Symptoms: Chest pain (angina), shortness of breath, fatigue
Diagnosis: ECG, Echocardiogram, Stress test, Coronary angiography
Treatment:
- Medications: Aspirin, Statins, Beta-blockers, Nitroglycerin, ACE inhibitors
- Procedures: Angioplasty with stent, Coronary artery bypass surgery (CABG)

--- Heart Attack / Myocardial Infarction (النوبة القلبية) ---
Definition: Complete blockage of blood flow to part of the heart muscle
Symptoms: Severe chest pain (pressure, squeezing), pain radiating to left arm/jaw/back, shortness of breath, sweating, nausea
EMERGENCY: Call ambulance immediately
Treatment: Aspirin (chew 325 mg immediately), thrombolytics, emergency angioplasty
Time is critical: "Time is muscle" — treatment within 90 minutes is ideal

--- Heart Failure (فشل القلب) ---
Definition: Heart cannot pump blood efficiently to meet the body's needs
Types: Left-sided (pulmonary congestion), Right-sided (peripheral edema), Systolic vs Diastolic
Symptoms: Shortness of breath, fatigue, swollen legs/ankles, rapid heartbeat, persistent cough
Diagnosis: BNP blood test, Echocardiogram, Chest X-ray
Treatment: ACE inhibitors, Beta-blockers, Diuretics (Furosemide), Spironolactone, Salt/fluid restriction

--- Arrhythmias (اضطرابات نظم القلب) ---
Types: Atrial fibrillation (AFib), Tachycardia, Bradycardia, Ventricular fibrillation
Symptoms: Palpitations, dizziness, fainting, chest discomfort
Diagnosis: ECG, Holter monitor, Electrophysiology study
Treatment: Anti-arrhythmic drugs (Amiodarone), Blood thinners (Warfarin, Rivaroxaban), Cardioversion, Pacemaker, Ablation

--- High Cholesterol (ارتفاع الكوليسترول) ---
Types:
- LDL ("bad" cholesterol): Should be < 100 mg/dL (high risk), < 130 mg/dL (general)
- HDL ("good" cholesterol): Should be > 40 mg/dL (men), > 50 mg/dL (women)
- Total Cholesterol: Should be < 200 mg/dL
- Triglycerides: Should be < 150 mg/dL
Treatment:
- Statins: Atorvastatin, Rosuvastatin (first-line)
- Lifestyle: Low-fat diet, exercise, weight loss, quit smoking
- Other medications: Ezetimibe, PCSK9 inhibitors for resistant cases
