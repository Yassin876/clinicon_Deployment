Topic: Dental and Oral Health (صحة الأسنان والفم)
Category: Dentistry

--- Toothache / Dental Pain (ألم الأسنان) ---
Definition: Pain in or around a tooth, often caused by decay, infection, or damage
Common Causes:
- Dental caries (tooth decay/التسوس): Bacteria erode enamel → dentin → pulp
- Pulpitis (التهاب عصب السن): Inflammation of the tooth pulp — can be reversible or irreversible
- Dental abscess (خراج الأسنان): Infection at root tip causing severe throbbing pain
- Cracked/fractured tooth (كسر السن): Pain on biting or with temperature changes
- Wisdom tooth problems (مشاكل ضرس العقل): Impaction, pericoronitis
Symptoms by cause:
- Decay: Sensitivity to sweet, hot, cold; pain when eating
- Pulpitis: Spontaneous sharp pain, worse at night, lingering sensitivity
- Abscess: Severe constant pain, swelling, fever, bad taste
- Cracked tooth: Sharp pain when biting, sensitivity to temperature
When to see a dentist URGENTLY:
- Swelling in face or jaw
- Fever with tooth pain
- Pain lasting more than 2 days
- Difficulty opening mouth or swallowing
Temporary relief:
- Paracetamol or Ibuprofen for pain
- Warm salt water rinse (half teaspoon in glass of warm water)
- Avoid very hot, cold, or sweet foods
- Clove oil on cotton ball applied to painful area

--- Tooth Pain When Eating (ألم الأسنان عند الأكل) ---
Definition: Pain triggered specifically by chewing or eating
Most common causes:
- Dental caries (التسوس): Cavity exposing sensitive dentin; pain with sweet/hot/cold food
- Cracked tooth syndrome: Hairline crack causes sharp pain on biting
- Loose or damaged filling: Exposes underlying tooth structure
- Gum recession: Exposed root surfaces cause sensitivity
- Periapical abscess: Deep infection causes pain on pressure
Diagnosis: Dental X-ray, bite test, cold test, visual exam
Treatment depends on cause:
- Cavity: Dental filling (حشو)
- Deep decay: Root canal treatment (علاج عصب)
- Cracked tooth: Crown or extraction depending on severity
- Abscess: Antibiotics + root canal or extraction
Advice for patients:
- Don't ignore pain when eating — it usually means the tooth needs treatment
- Chew on the other side temporarily
- See a dentist as soon as possible — delaying makes it worse
- Specialist needed: طبيب أسنان (Dentist)

--- Gum Disease (أمراض اللثة) ---
Types:
- Gingivitis (التهاب اللثة): Early stage, reversible with good hygiene
- Periodontitis (التهاب دواعم السن): Advanced, can cause tooth loss
Symptoms: Red swollen gums, bleeding when brushing, bad breath, receding gums, loose teeth
Risk factors: Poor oral hygiene, smoking, diabetes, genetic predisposition
Treatment:
- Professional cleaning (scaling and polishing)
- Deep cleaning (root planing) for periodontitis
- Antibiotics for infection
- Surgery for advanced cases
- Daily brushing (twice) and flossing
Prevention:
- Brush teeth twice daily with fluoride toothpaste
- Floss daily
- Regular dental checkups every 6 months
- Quit smoking

--- Tooth Sensitivity (حساسية الأسنان) ---
Definition: Sharp brief pain when teeth exposed to hot, cold, sweet, or acidic stimuli
Causes: Worn enamel, exposed roots, cavities, cracked teeth, recent dental procedures
Treatment:
- Desensitizing toothpaste (e.g., Sensodyne)
- Fluoride application at dentist
- Dental bonding for exposed roots
- Root canal for severe cases
Prevention: Soft-bristle brush, avoid acidic foods, don't brush too hard

--- Wisdom Teeth Problems (مشاكل ضرس العقل) ---
Common issues: Impaction, pericoronitis (gum infection around partially erupted tooth), crowding
Symptoms: Pain at back of jaw, swelling, difficulty opening mouth, bad taste
Treatment:
- Pericoronitis: Warm salt water rinse, antibiotics, removal of tooth
- Impacted: Surgical extraction
- Not all wisdom teeth need removal — only if causing problems
When to see dentist: Pain, swelling, difficulty chewing at back of mouth

--- Dental Emergency First Aid (إسعافات أولية للأسنان) ---
Knocked-out tooth:
- Hold by crown (not root)
- Rinse gently with milk or saline
- Try to place back in socket OR store in milk
- See dentist within 30 minutes
Broken tooth:
- Rinse mouth with warm water
- Apply cold compress for swelling
- See dentist same day
Severe toothache:
- Rinse with warm salt water
- Take Paracetamol (not Aspirin directly on gum)
- See dentist as soon as possible
