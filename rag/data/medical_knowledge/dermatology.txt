Topic: Skin Diseases (الأمراض الجلدية)
Category: Dermatology

--- Eczema / Atopic Dermatitis (الإكزيما) ---
Definition: Chronic inflammatory skin condition causing itchy, red, dry patches
Common in: Children (often improves with age), people with allergies or asthma
Triggers: Dry skin, irritants (soaps, detergents), allergens, stress, heat
Treatment:
- Moisturizers (emollients) — apply frequently
- Topical corticosteroids: Hydrocortisone (mild), Betamethasone (moderate-severe)
- Topical calcineurin inhibitors: Tacrolimus, Pimecrolimus
- Antihistamines for itching: Cetirizine, Loratadine
- Avoid triggers, short lukewarm showers

--- Psoriasis (الصدفية) ---
Definition: Autoimmune condition causing rapid skin cell turnover, forming thick scaly patches
Symptoms: Red patches with silvery scales, itching, dry cracked skin, nail changes
Types: Plaque (most common), Guttate, Inverse, Pustular, Erythrodermic
Treatment:
- Mild: Topical corticosteroids, Vitamin D analogs (Calcipotriol), Coal tar
- Moderate: Phototherapy (UVB), Methotrexate
- Severe: Biologics (Adalimumab, Secukinumab, Ustekinumab)

--- Acne (حب الشباب) ---
Causes: Excess sebum, clogged pores, bacteria (C. acnes), hormonal changes
Types: Comedonal (blackheads/whiteheads), Inflammatory (papules/pustules), Cystic
Treatment:
- Mild: Benzoyl peroxide, Topical retinoids (Adapalene), Salicylic acid
- Moderate: Topical antibiotics (Clindamycin) + Benzoyl peroxide
- Severe/Cystic: Oral antibiotics (Doxycycline), Isotretinoin (Accutane)
- Hormonal: Oral contraceptives, Spironolactone (females)
- Don't pick or squeeze, gentle cleansing, non-comedogenic products

--- Fungal Infections (العدوى الفطرية) ---
Tinea Corporis (Ringworm): Ring-shaped rash. Treatment: Topical antifungals (Clotrimazole, Terbinafine)
Tinea Pedis (Athlete's Foot): Between toes, itching, peeling. Treatment: Antifungal cream, keep feet dry
Tinea Cruris (Jock Itch): Groin area. Treatment: Antifungal cream, loose clothing
Candidiasis: Oral thrush or skin fold infection. Treatment: Nystatin, Fluconazole
Onychomycosis (Nail Fungus): Thick, discolored nails. Treatment: Oral Terbinafine (3-6 months)

--- Urticaria / Hives (الأرتيكاريا) ---
Definition: Itchy raised welts on the skin caused by histamine release
Causes: Allergic reactions, medications, infections, stress, idiopathic
Treatment:
- Antihistamines: Cetirizine, Loratadine, Fexofenadine (non-sedating)
- Severe: Oral corticosteroids short course
- Anaphylaxis: Epinephrine (EpiPen) — emergency
- Chronic: Omalizumab for refractory cases
