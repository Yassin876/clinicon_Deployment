Topic: Headache and Head Pain (الصداع وألم الرأس)
Category: Neurology / General

--- Tension Headache (صداع التوتر) ---
Definition: Most common type of headache, feels like a tight band around the head
Causes: Stress, anxiety, poor posture, eye strain, dehydration, lack of sleep, skipping meals
Symptoms: Dull aching pain on both sides, tightness in forehead/temples/back of head, not pulsating
Duration: 30 minutes to several days
Treatment:
- Paracetamol or Ibuprofen (don't overuse — max 2-3 days/week)
- Rest in a quiet dark room
- Neck and shoulder stretches
- Adequate hydration and regular meals
- Stress management techniques
Prevention:
- Regular sleep schedule
- Exercise regularly
- Take breaks from screens every 30-60 minutes
- Good posture at desk
- Manage stress
When to see a doctor: If headaches are daily or very frequent
Specialist needed: طبيب أعصاب (Neurologist) or طبيب عام (General Practitioner)

--- Migraine (الصداع النصفي) ---
Definition: Recurring headache disorder with moderate to severe throbbing pain, usually one-sided
Triggers: Stress, hormonal changes, certain foods (chocolate, cheese, caffeine), sleep changes, bright lights, strong smells
Symptoms:
- Throbbing/pulsating pain, usually one side
- Nausea and vomiting
- Sensitivity to light and sound
- Lasts 4-72 hours
- Some people get aura (visual disturbances like flashing lights) before the headache
Treatment:
- Acute: Paracetamol + Ibuprofen early, Triptans (Sumatriptan) for moderate-severe
- Rest in dark quiet room
- Cold compress on forehead
- Prevention (if >= 4 attacks/month): Propranolol, Amitriptyline, Topiramate
- Keep a headache diary to identify triggers
Advice for patients:
- Take painkillers at the very start of the headache, don't wait
- Avoid known triggers
- Regular sleep and meals help prevent attacks
Specialist needed: طبيب أعصاب (Neurologist)

--- Sinus Headache (صداع الجيوب الأنفية) ---
Definition: Headache caused by sinus inflammation or infection (sinusitis)
Causes: Sinus infection, allergies, cold/flu, nasal polyps
Symptoms:
- Pain and pressure around eyes, cheeks, forehead
- Worse when bending forward
- Nasal congestion and runny nose
- May have fever and thick nasal discharge
- Pain worse in the morning
Treatment:
- Steam inhalation
- Saline nasal spray
- Decongestants (short-term only)
- Paracetamol or Ibuprofen for pain
- Antibiotics if bacterial infection (prescribed by doctor)
Prevention:
- Treat allergies
- Use humidifier
- Stay hydrated
Specialist needed: طبيب أنف وأذن وحنجرة (ENT) or طبيب عام (GP)

--- Cluster Headache (الصداع العنقودي) ---
Definition: Severe one-sided headache attacks occurring in clusters/groups over weeks or months
Symptoms:
- Excruciating pain around one eye or temple
- Eye watering and redness on affected side
- Nasal congestion on affected side
- Restlessness (can't lie still)
- Attacks last 15 minutes to 3 hours
- Can occur 1-8 times daily during cluster period
Treatment:
- Acute: High-flow oxygen (100%), Sumatriptan injection
- Prevention: Verapamil, short course of Prednisolone
- NOT responsive to regular painkillers
When to see a doctor: Immediately — needs specialist diagnosis
Specialist needed: طبيب أعصاب (Neurologist)

--- Headache with Fever (صداع مع سخونة) ---
Possible causes:
- Common cold or flu (most common)
- COVID-19
- Sinusitis
- Meningitis (EMERGENCY — stiff neck, sensitivity to light, rash)
- Other infections
When to go to EMERGENCY:
- Severe headache with stiff neck and fever
- Rash that doesn't fade when pressed
- Confusion or drowsiness
- Sensitivity to bright light with fever
Treatment: Depends on cause — see a doctor if fever persists more than 3 days

--- Headache from Eye Strain (صداع إجهاد العين) ---
Causes: Prolonged screen use, reading in poor light, uncorrected vision problems
Symptoms: Dull pain around eyes and forehead, worse with continued activity, eye fatigue
Treatment:
- 20-20-20 rule: Every 20 minutes, look at something 20 feet away for 20 seconds
- Proper lighting
- Get eyes checked — may need glasses
- Adjust screen brightness and distance
Specialist needed: طبيب عيون (Ophthalmologist) if vision problems suspected

--- When to Seek URGENT Medical Help for Headache ---
Go to emergency if:
- "Worst headache of my life" — sudden severe onset (thunderclap headache)
- Headache with fever, stiff neck, and rash → possible meningitis
- Headache after head injury
- Headache with confusion, weakness, vision loss, or speech problems → possible stroke
- Headache that wakes you from sleep repeatedly
- New headache pattern after age 50
- Headache with seizures
These could indicate serious conditions like meningitis, brain hemorrhage, or stroke

--- General Headache Advice (نصائح عامة للصداع) ---
- Drink enough water (at least 8 glasses daily)
- Don't skip meals
- Get 7-8 hours of sleep regularly
- Limit caffeine — too much OR sudden withdrawal both cause headaches
- Exercise regularly (30 minutes, 5 days/week)
- Don't overuse painkillers — taking them more than 2-3 days/week can cause rebound headaches
- Keep a headache diary: note when, how long, what you ate, stress level
- If headaches are frequent (more than 15 days/month), see a neurologist
