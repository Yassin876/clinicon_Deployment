Topic: Musculoskeletal Conditions (أمراض العظام والمفاصل)
Category: Orthopedics

--- Osteoarthritis (خشونة المفاصل) ---
Definition: Degenerative joint disease where cartilage wears down over time
Common Sites: Knees, hips, hands, spine
Risk Factors: Age, obesity, joint injuries, repetitive stress, genetics
Symptoms: Joint pain, stiffness (especially morning), swelling, reduced range of motion, crepitus
Diagnosis: X-ray (joint space narrowing, osteophytes), clinical examination
Treatment:
- Weight loss, physical therapy, exercise (low-impact: swimming, cycling)
- Paracetamol (first-line for mild pain)
- NSAIDs: Ibuprofen, Diclofenac (topical or oral)
- Intra-articular corticosteroid injections
- Hyaluronic acid injections
- Joint replacement surgery for severe cases

--- Osteoporosis (هشاشة العظام) ---
Definition: Decreased bone density making bones fragile and prone to fractures
Risk Factors: Postmenopausal women, age, low calcium, vitamin D deficiency, sedentary lifestyle, smoking, steroids
Diagnosis: DEXA scan (T-score: Normal > -1, Osteopenia -1 to -2.5, Osteoporosis < -2.5)
Treatment:
- Calcium supplements: 1000-1200 mg/day
- Vitamin D: 800-1000 IU/day
- Bisphosphonates: Alendronate, Risedronate (first-line)
- Weight-bearing exercises
- Fall prevention strategies

--- Low Back Pain (ألم أسفل الظهر) ---
Causes: Muscle strain, disc herniation, degenerative disc disease, spinal stenosis, poor posture
Red Flags: Bowel/bladder dysfunction, progressive weakness, saddle anesthesia, fever, unexplained weight loss
Treatment:
- Acute: Stay active (bed rest NOT recommended), NSAIDs, muscle relaxants
- Physical therapy and core strengthening exercises
- Chronic: Multidisciplinary approach, cognitive behavioral therapy, epidural steroid injections
- Surgery: Only for specific indications (cauda equina, progressive neurological deficit)

--- Rheumatoid Arthritis (الروماتويد) ---
Definition: Autoimmune disease causing chronic inflammation of joints
Symptoms: Symmetric joint pain and swelling (hands, wrists, feet), morning stiffness > 1 hour, fatigue, rheumatoid nodules
Diagnosis: RF (Rheumatoid Factor), Anti-CCP antibodies, ESR, CRP, X-ray
Treatment:
- DMARDs: Methotrexate (first-line), Hydroxychloroquine, Sulfasalazine
- Biologics: Adalimumab, Etanercept, Infliximab
- NSAIDs and corticosteroids for symptom relief
- Early treatment is critical to prevent joint destruction

--- Gout (النقرس) ---
Definition: Inflammatory arthritis caused by uric acid crystal deposition in joints
Symptoms: Sudden severe pain, redness, swelling — usually big toe (podagra)
Diagnosis: Serum uric acid (> 7 mg/dL), joint fluid analysis showing crystals
Treatment:
- Acute attack: Colchicine, NSAIDs (Indomethacin), Corticosteroids
- Prevention: Allopurinol, Febuxostat (lower uric acid)
- Diet: Avoid red meat, organ meats, shellfish, alcohol (especially beer)
- Drink plenty of water
