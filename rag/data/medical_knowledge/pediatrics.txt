Topic: Common Pediatric Conditions (أمراض الأطفال الشائعة)
Category: Pediatrics

--- Fever in Children (الحمى عند الأطفال) ---
Normal temperature: 36.5-37.5°C
Fever: >= 38°C
When to worry: < 3 months with fever >= 38°C (emergency), persistent > 3 days, febrile seizures, poor feeding
Treatment:
- Paracetamol (15 mg/kg every 4-6 hours) — safe from birth
- Ibuprofen (10 mg/kg every 6-8 hours) — from 6 months
- Fluids, light clothing, lukewarm sponging
- NEVER give Aspirin to children (Reye's syndrome risk)

--- Tonsillitis (التهاب اللوزتين) ---
Causes: Viral (most common), Bacterial (Group A Streptococcus)
Symptoms: Sore throat, difficulty swallowing, fever, swollen tonsils, white patches
Diagnosis: Rapid strep test, throat culture
Treatment:
- Viral: Supportive (fluids, rest, Paracetamol)
- Bacterial (Strep): Amoxicillin 50 mg/kg/day for 10 days, or Penicillin V
- Tonsillectomy: If recurrent (>7 episodes/year) or sleep apnea

--- Otitis Media (التهاب الأذن الوسطى) ---
Very common in children < 5 years
Symptoms: Ear pain, fever, irritability, pulling at ear, hearing difficulty
Treatment:
- Mild: Watch and wait 48-72 hours with pain relief
- Moderate/Severe: Amoxicillin 80-90 mg/kg/day for 5-10 days
- Recurrent: Referral to ENT, possible tympanostomy tubes

--- Gastroenteritis in Children (النزلة المعوية) ---
Most common cause: Rotavirus (vaccine available), Norovirus
Danger: Dehydration — the main concern
Signs of dehydration: Dry mouth, no tears, sunken eyes, reduced urine, lethargy
Treatment:
- ORS (Oral Rehydration Solution) — small frequent sips
- Continue breastfeeding
- BRAT diet when tolerating food
- Zinc supplements (10-20 mg/day for 10-14 days — WHO recommendation)
- Seek emergency if: bloody stool, severe dehydration, persistent vomiting, < 6 months

--- Childhood Vaccination Schedule (جدول التطعيمات) ---
Birth: BCG, Hepatitis B (1st dose)
2 months: DTaP, IPV, Hib, PCV, Rotavirus, Hepatitis B (2nd dose)
4 months: DTaP, IPV, Hib, PCV, Rotavirus
6 months: DTaP, IPV, Hib, PCV, Hepatitis B (3rd dose), Influenza (annual)
12 months: MMR (1st dose), Varicella, Hepatitis A, PCV booster
18 months: DTaP booster, Hib booster
4-6 years: DTaP, IPV, MMR (2nd dose), Varicella (2nd dose)
Note: Schedule may vary by country. Always follow local health authority guidelines.

--- Common Childhood Rashes ---
Measles: High fever, cough, runny nose, then rash (head to toe). Vaccine preventable.
Chickenpox: Itchy vesicular rash, fever. Vaccine available. Calamine lotion, Acyclovir if severe.
Hand-Foot-Mouth Disease: Blisters on hands, feet, mouth. Viral (Coxsackievirus). Supportive treatment.
Roseola: High fever 3-5 days then rash appears as fever breaks. Benign, supportive care.
