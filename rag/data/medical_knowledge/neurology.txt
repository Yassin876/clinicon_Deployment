Topic: Neurological Conditions (أمراض الجهاز العصبي)
Category: Neurology

--- Migraine (الصداع النصفي) ---
Definition: Recurring headache disorder with moderate to severe throbbing pain, usually one-sided
Phases: Prodrome, Aura (visual/sensory disturbances — in 25%), Headache, Postdrome
Triggers: Stress, hormonal changes, certain foods (chocolate, cheese, caffeine), sleep changes, bright lights
Symptoms: Throbbing headache, nausea, vomiting, sensitivity to light and sound, lasting 4-72 hours
Treatment:
- Acute: Paracetamol + NSAIDs (Ibuprofen), Triptans (Sumatriptan) for moderate-severe
- Prevention (if >= 4 attacks/month): Propranolol, Amitriptyline, Topiramate, Valproate
- Lifestyle: Regular sleep, avoid triggers, stress management
- CGRP inhibitors: Erenumab (newer preventive option)

--- Epilepsy (الصرع) ---
Definition: Neurological disorder characterized by recurrent unprovoked seizures
Types: Focal (partial), Generalized (absence, tonic-clonic), Unknown onset
Diagnosis: EEG (electroencephalogram), MRI brain, clinical history
Treatment:
- First-line: Levetiracetam, Valproate, Carbamazepine, Lamotrigine
- Choice depends on seizure type and patient factors
- Medication adherence is critical — don't stop suddenly
- Surgery for drug-resistant focal epilepsy
First Aid for Seizures:
- Stay calm, protect from injury, don't put anything in the mouth
- Turn on side (recovery position), time the seizure
- Call emergency if seizure lasts > 5 minutes or first seizure

--- Stroke (السكتة الدماغية) ---
Types:
- Ischemic (87%): Blood clot blocks brain artery
- Hemorrhagic (13%): Brain blood vessel bursts
FAST Signs: Face drooping, Arm weakness, Speech difficulty, Time to call emergency
EMERGENCY: Every minute counts — "Time is brain"
Treatment:
- Ischemic: tPA (clot-busting drug) within 4.5 hours, Thrombectomy within 24 hours
- Hemorrhagic: Blood pressure control, surgery if needed
- Prevention: Control hypertension, diabetes, cholesterol; antiplatelet (Aspirin, Clopidogrel); anticoagulants for AFib

--- Alzheimer's Disease (الزهايمر) ---
Definition: Progressive neurodegenerative disease causing memory loss and cognitive decline
Stages: Mild (forgetfulness), Moderate (confusion, behavioral changes), Severe (full dependence)
Diagnosis: Clinical assessment, cognitive testing (MMSE, MoCA), MRI brain, PET scan
Treatment:
- Cholinesterase inhibitors: Donepezil, Rivastigmine, Galantamine
- NMDA antagonist: Memantine (moderate-severe)
- No cure yet — treatment slows progression
- Supportive care, caregiver support, structured routine

--- Parkinson's Disease (الشلل الرعاش) ---
Definition: Progressive movement disorder due to dopamine deficiency in the brain
Symptoms: Tremor at rest, rigidity, bradykinesia (slow movement), postural instability
Diagnosis: Clinical (no definitive test), DaTscan
Treatment:
- Levodopa/Carbidopa (most effective)
- Dopamine agonists: Pramipexole, Ropinirole
- MAO-B inhibitors: Selegiline, Rasagiline
- Physical therapy, exercise, speech therapy
- Deep brain stimulation (DBS) for advanced cases
