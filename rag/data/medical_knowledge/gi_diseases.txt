Topic: Gastrointestinal Diseases (أمراض الجهاز الهضمي)
Category: Gastroenterology

--- GERD / Acid Reflux (ارتجاع المريء) ---
Definition: Stomach acid frequently flows back into the esophagus
Symptoms: Heartburn, regurgitation, chest pain, difficulty swallowing, chronic cough
Treatment:
- Lifestyle: Avoid spicy/fatty food, don't eat before bed, elevate head while sleeping
- Antacids: Gaviscon, Maalox (quick relief)
- H2 Blockers: Ranitidine, Famotidine
- PPIs: Omeprazole, Esomeprazole, Pantoprazole (most effective)

--- Peptic Ulcer Disease (قرحة المعدة) ---
Causes: H. pylori infection (most common), NSAIDs overuse, stress
Symptoms: Burning stomach pain, bloating, nausea, vomiting, dark stools (bleeding)
Diagnosis: Upper GI endoscopy, H. pylori breath test or stool antigen test
Treatment:
- H. pylori triple therapy: PPI + Amoxicillin + Clarithromycin (14 days)
- Stop NSAIDs
- PPI for 4-8 weeks
- Sucralfate for mucosal protection

--- Irritable Bowel Syndrome / IBS (القولون العصبي) ---
Definition: Functional GI disorder with no structural abnormality
Symptoms: Abdominal pain, bloating, diarrhea and/or constipation, gas
Diagnosis: Clinical diagnosis (Rome IV criteria), exclude other causes
Treatment:
- Diet: Low FODMAP diet, increase fiber gradually
- Antispasmodics: Mebeverine, Hyoscine
- For diarrhea: Loperamide
- For constipation: Lactulose, Polyethylene glycol
- Stress management, probiotics

--- Gastroenteritis (نزلة معوية) ---
Causes: Viral (Rotavirus, Norovirus), Bacterial (Salmonella, E. coli), Parasitic
Symptoms: Diarrhea, vomiting, abdominal cramps, fever, dehydration
Treatment:
- Oral Rehydration Solution (ORS) — most important
- Clear fluids, BRAT diet (Bananas, Rice, Applesauce, Toast)
- Antiemetics: Ondansetron for severe vomiting
- Antibiotics ONLY for confirmed bacterial infection
- Seek medical care if: bloody diarrhea, high fever, severe dehydration, symptoms > 3 days

--- Hepatitis (التهاب الكبد) ---
Types:
- Hepatitis A: Fecal-oral transmission, usually self-limiting, vaccine available
- Hepatitis B: Blood/body fluids, can become chronic, vaccine available. Treatment: Tenofovir, Entecavir
- Hepatitis C: Blood-borne, often chronic. Treatment: Direct-acting antivirals (Sofosbuvir + Daclatasvir) — cure rate > 95%
Diagnosis: Liver function tests (ALT, AST), Hepatitis serology, viral load
Complications: Cirrhosis, liver failure, hepatocellular carcinoma
